# Indicius — Human-Centered Design Agency Landing Page

Figma dizayni asosida to'liq yaratilgan **Indicius** (Digital Design & Branding Agency) veb-sahifasi.

🔗 **Figma dizayn havolasi:** [Indicius Figma Design](https://www.figma.com/file/HmHAcBf0rIuGA3Zh7TsSvR/Indicius?type=design&node-id=1%3A2&mode=design&t=E6t9XcV1tVqDiMrJ-1)  
🐙 **GitHub Repozitoriy:** [https://github.com/soxibs48-eng/amaliy6](https://github.com/soxibs48-eng/amaliy6)  
▲ **Vercel Deploy:** [Vercel 1-Click Deploy](https://vercel.com/new/clone?repository-url=https://github.com/soxibs48-eng/amaliy6)

---

## 🎨 Loyiha xususiyatlari

- **Zamonaviy tipografiya & ranglar palitrasi**: Google Fonts (`DM Sans` & `Archivo`), Indicius brend ranglari (`#3098F3`, `#FFCC00`, `#05051E`, `#FF5B60`).
- **Responsive dizayn**: Desktop, planshet va mobil telefonlar uchun 100% moslashuvchan interfeys.
- **Interaktiv xususiyatlar**:
  - Mobil menyu (Drawer navigatsiya).
  - Interaktiv xizmatlar (Services) tab selektori: *Design Sprint*, *Branding*, *Digital Products*.
  - Haqiqiy keyslar (Salsa & Operators Guild) vizuallari bilan.
  - "Meet the Humans" — 10 nafar jamoa a'zolarining interaktiv suzuvchi avatarlari.
  - Jonli xabarnoma bilan obuna bo'lish (Newsletter) formasi.
  - "Let's chat" interaktiv aloqa modali.
- **Vercel uchun tayyor**: `vercel.json` orqali bir martada avtomatik deploy qilinadi.

## 🌐 Vercel da deploy qilish

1. [https://vercel.com](https://vercel.com) ga kiring.
2. **Add New...** -> **Project** tugmasini bosing.
3. GitHub hisobingizdagi **`amaliy6`** repozitoriysini tanlang.
4. **Deploy** tugmasini bosing — 10 soniyada jonli veb-sayt havolasi tayyor bo'ladi!
